HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: the ball starts on the teal tile and must reach the gold exit.
  • Arrows: each arrow sends the ball to the next tile. Amber arrows turn when you tap them. Pale arrows are fixed.
  • Steps: the ball can climb or drop one level at a time. Any bigger step fails.
  • Run: press Run route to send the ball. A failed run shows the reason and resets.
  • Stars: 3 at par or better, 2 within par + 2, 1 for finishing.
  • Keys: Enter runs, Z undoes, R resets, Esc pauses. Left-drag rotates the camera.
